IT & Digital Skills

Basic Computer Applications

This illustrative programme introduces the parts of a computer, basic operating-system navigation and common productivity tasks. Example activities could include organising folders, creating a document and understanding safe device use. The emphasis is on building familiarity through simple, practical exercises.

Illustrative learning topics

  • Computer and operating-system basics
  • Files, folders and document creation
  • Responsible and safe computer use
Demonstration example only. Availability, eligibility, duration, fees and any certification must be confirmed by the Trust. No enrolment is offered through this demo.

Introduction to Electrical Skills

This demonstration overview introduces electrical terminology, identification of basic tools and the importance of safe working procedures. Illustrative learning topics

Employability & Communication Skills

Workplace Communication

This proposed programme explores how people share information respectfully and effectively at work. Sample exercises could include summarising instructions, drafting a professional message and participating in a group discussion. Feedback would help learners reflect on clarity, listening and tone.

Illustrative learning topics

  • Active listening and clear expression
  • Professional messages and conversations
  • Teamwork and respectful communication

  • Professional responses and service etiquette
